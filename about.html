About This Site

Enterprise Information System Technology Hub · eis-w.com

What this site is

eis-w.com is a bilingual (English and Chinese) technology site about building browser-based enterprise information systems (EIS). It documents the engineering decisions, data models and operating rules that come out of real EIS delivery work — portals, master data, documents, process, integration and security — the kind of knowledge usually scattered across project archives and hallway conversations.

The content is organized into six sections: Portal & Collaboration, Master Data, Document & Knowledge, Process & BPM, Data Integration and Security & Permissions.

Editorial stance

  • Practice over theory. Every article describes something that has been built and run, including where the naive approach fails.
  • Boring technology, clearly explained. We prefer a data model that works over an architecture diagram that impresses.
  • Bilingual by default. Every article is published in English and Chinese. English is the default; the Chinese version lives under /zh/.

Technical choices

This site follows the philosophy it preaches: pure static HTML with a single stylesheet, zero JavaScript, and anchor pagination. Pages load instantly on any connection, and there is nothing to break.
